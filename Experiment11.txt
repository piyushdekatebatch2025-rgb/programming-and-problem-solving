Q11.1.1 Write a Python program that reads an integer, calculates the sum of its digits, and prints the result.
num = int(input("Enter a number: "))
sum_digits = 0
temp = abs(num)
while temp > 0:
    sum_digits += temp % 10
    temp //= 10
print("Sum of digits:",   sum_digits  )
